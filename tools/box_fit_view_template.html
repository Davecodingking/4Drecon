Point clouds and fitted boxes

Each object is measured from its 8 frames with the largest mask. Points are colored by frame. The blue box is what the pipeline fits after merging all 8 frames; orange boxes are fitted to one frame at a time. A static object's per-frame boxes should stack; when they spread out, the object moved, or the depth or the track is wrong. Green boxes are where each object ended up after the physics pass — measured off the mesh in the final glb, the one the video is rendered from. Dashed boxes were recalled by WildDet3D after SAM 3 missed them — no masks, so no points. People come from SAM 3D Body: one box per video frame, placed exactly as in the animated scene. Drag the timeline to move them; the camera frustum follows the same frame.

Spread = largest horizontal distance between the per-frame centroids. Under 0.3 m is ordinary depth noise. 0.3–1.5 m: the object moved, or its mask or depth drifts between frames. Over 1.5 m: the track almost certainly jumped to a different object. Recalled instances have no spread and sort last.
